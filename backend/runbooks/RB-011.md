# Customer incident communication policy

Applies to every service. This policy covers **what we tell customers during an
incident, and when**. It does not cover who to page internally — see the on-call
escalation policy for that.

## The principle

We tell customers early, in plain language, and we say what we do not yet know.
A vague early notice is better than a precise late one. The cost of telling
customers about an incident that turns out to be minor is far lower than the
cost of a customer discovering a major one on their own.

## When a public notice is required

| Situation | Notice required |
|---|---|
| SEV1, any duration | Yes, immediately |
| SEV2 lasting more than 30 minutes | Yes |
| SEV2 resolved within 30 minutes | Status page only, no direct contact |
| SEV3 | No |
| Any incident involving customer data | Yes, immediately, and notify legal |

Payment failures always require a notice regardless of duration, because a
customer whose payment failed will assume the fault is theirs.

## Who writes it

The **incident commander** owns communication. If no commander has been
appointed, it is the responder who owns it — and that is itself a reason to
escalate, so that someone else can take communications while you keep working.

Engineering drafts, support reviews for tone, and for SEV1 the duty
communications lead approves before publication. This review is capped at ten
minutes. If the reviewer cannot be reached in ten minutes, publish anyway.

## What a notice contains

1. What customers are experiencing, in their words, not ours. "Checkout is
   failing" — not "elevated 5xx on the checkout service".
2. When it started.
3. What we are doing about it.
4. When we will next update, and then actually update at that time even if there
   is nothing new to say.

Never include internal hostnames, dashboard links, stack traces, or the name of
the engineer who deployed the change.

## Cadence

Update every 30 minutes during a SEV1 and every 60 minutes during a SEV2, until
resolved. A final notice confirms resolution and states plainly whether any
customer action is needed.

## Afterwards

For any SEV1, a public summary is published within five working days. It states
what happened, the impact, and what we are changing. It does not name
individuals.
