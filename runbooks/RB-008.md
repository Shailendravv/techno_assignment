# Runbook: inventory-api — Stock sync lag

## Symptoms

The `inventory_sync_lag_seconds` gauge exceeds 900. Stock levels shown on
product pages disagree with the warehouse system. Oversell reports arrive from
customer support — an order accepted for an item that is actually out of stock.
The `inventory-sync-lag` alert fires.

## Background

Stock levels reach inventory-api through a Kafka topic published by the
warehouse management system. inventory-api consumes `wms.stock.v2` and applies
deltas. Lag means the consumer is behind the producer, so the API is serving
stale counts.

## First checks

1. Check consumer group lag for the `inventory-sync` group. Identify whether lag
   is spread across partitions or concentrated in one. A single hot partition
   usually means one SKU with extreme update frequency.
2. Check whether the consumer is alive at all. A crash-looping consumer shows
   lag that grows perfectly linearly.
3. Check for a schema change on the topic. A producer that starts emitting a
   field the consumer cannot deserialise will stall the partition rather than
   skip the message.

## Mitigation

If the consumer is crash-looping, restart it — but capture the logs first. The
stack trace is the whole diagnosis, and it is gone after a restart.

If one partition is hot, increase consumer concurrency for that partition group.

If a schema change is the cause, this cannot be fixed from the inventory side.
Escalate to the warehouse integration team. Do not skip the offending offsets
without their agreement: skipping a stock delta means the count stays wrong
permanently rather than temporarily.

## Preventing oversell while lagging

If lag exceeds 1800 seconds, enable the `inventory.conservative_stock` flag. It
subtracts a safety buffer from displayed stock, trading a small number of lost
sales for avoiding oversell. Turn it off once lag is below 300.

## Escalation

Oversell has customer and finance impact. If lag is growing rather than
shrinking after mitigation, escalate to the Inventory team's secondary on-call
and notify the warehouse integration team. See the escalation policy.
